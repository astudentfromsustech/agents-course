## Answer

As of 2026-09-30, LLM bioinformatics agents have moved from chat-based workflow suggestion to executable planning, code generation, environment/tool use, debugging, and artifact checking. Newer systems include broad biomedical agents (STELLA, Biomni), scientific tool orchestrators (SciToolAgent), autonomous single-cell/spatial analysts (CellVoyager, SpatialAgent), and BioMaster, a general multi-agent pipeline builder explicitly evaluated on Hi-C. [https://github.com/zaixizhang/STELLA](https://github.com/zaixizhang/STELLA) [https://github.com/HICAI-ZJU/SciToolAgent](https://github.com/HICAI-ZJU/SciToolAgent) [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-026-03029-6](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-026-03029-6) [https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666389926001200](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666389926001200)

The key distinction is that a 3D-genomics *workflow agent* now exists: BioMaster plans and executes a pairtools/cooler-based Hi-C workflow, reports full success in five GM12878 trials, and compares contact maps and Mustache loop calls with a manual pipeline. Its scope is conventional data processing/QC/matrix construction/loop calling, not mechanistic modelling. [https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666389926001200](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666389926001200)

I found no published, dedicated LLM agent that closes the loop from Hi-C/Micro-C/HiChIP/ChIA-PET observations to chromatin polymer or phase-separation/condensate simulations, evaluates simulation–contact agreement, and iteratively revises the physical model. This is an absence claim, so evidence is necessarily thin; it is qualified by BioMaster’s broad Hi-C automation and an apparently unpublished HiC-MCP tool-server project. [https://github.com/ai4nucleome/BioMaster](https://github.com/ai4nucleome/BioMaster) [https://github.com/javrodriguez](https://github.com/javrodriguez)

HiCFoundation is a relevant competitor but not an agent: it is a self-supervised predictor/representation model taking masked Hi-C submatrices and producing reconstructed matrices/embeddings and task-specific outputs, including reproducibility scoring, resolution enhancement, loop detection, epigenomic prediction, multi-species and single-cell analyses. [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-026-03097-8](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-026-03097-8) [https://github.com/Noble-Lab/HiCFoundation](https://github.com/Noble-Lab/HiCFoundation)

## Key findings

- **BioMaster (Patterns, 2026; code released)** is the closest direct competitor: Plan/Task/Debug/Check agents turn natural-language objectives into auditable workflows; its published evaluation covers 49 tasks, 102 tools, and includes a nine-step Hi-C workflow. The paper reports pairtools/cooler workflow execution, five-of-five stage completion, HiCRep SCC >0.99 against a manual GM12878 pipeline, identical Mustache loop counts, and matched APA score. [https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666389926001200](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666389926001200) Code and the paper-corresponding `master` branch are public. [https://github.com/ai4nucleome/BioMaster](https://github.com/ai4nucleome/BioMaster)

- **STELLA (bioRxiv, July 2025; code released)** is a general biomedical multi-agent system: manager, developer, critic, and tool-creation agents retrieve/create templates and run analyses, literature/database queries, virtual screening, and robotic-lab workflows. Its reported evidence includes tool-creation and public benchmarks plus AML CRISPR and enzyme-design case studies; these are author-reported rather than an independent bioinformatics benchmark. [https://github.com/zaixizhang/STELLA](https://github.com/zaixizhang/STELLA)

- **SciToolAgent (Nature Computational Science, 2025; code released)** is a general planner–executor–summarizer over a knowledge graph of 500+ scientific tools. It evaluates answer accuracy and prescribed tool-path accuracy through SciToolEval, with case studies primarily in protein engineering, chemistry, and materials—not 3D genomics. [https://github.com/HICAI-ZJU/SciToolAgent](https://github.com/HICAI-ZJU/SciToolAgent)

- **CellVoyager (Nature Methods, 2026; code/data released)** autonomously analyzes `.h5ad` single-cell data in a live notebook, proposes hypotheses, executes code, and exposes a human intervention interface. Its CellBench/case-study implementation and Zenodo archive are released. [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-026-03029-6](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-026-03029-6) [https://github.com/zou-group/CellVoyager](https://github.com/zou-group/CellVoyager)

- **SpatialAgent and STAgent (preprints/software; code released)** automate spatial-transcriptomics/scRNA-seq workflows, literature/database querying, analysis, report generation, and—in STAgent—spatial-domain identification and gene imputation. Neither concerns 3D chromatin contact data. [https://github.com/Genentech/SpatialAgent](https://github.com/Genentech/SpatialAgent) [https://github.com/LiuLab-Bioelectronics-Harvard/STAgent](https://github.com/LiuLab-Bioelectronics-Harvard/STAgent)

- **Finch/FutureHouse (Nature, 2026)** is an AI-scientist-style notebook agent evaluated on 170 BixBench drug-discovery-related questions, including RNA-seq, genomics, and epigenomics. This is an agentic workflow framework, not a 3D-genome system. [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10652-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10652-y)

- **HiCFoundation (Nature Methods, 2026; Apache-2.0 code/weights)** is the major 3D-genome foundation-model competitor. It was pretrained on hundreds of Hi-C assays / 118 million matrix patches; it reconstructs masked patches and is fine-tuned for loop detection, resolution enhancement, reproducibility, epigenomic prediction, 316-species analysis, and single-cell Hi-C. It does not plan tools, execute pipelines, or run physical simulations. [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-026-03097-8](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-026-03097-8) [https://github.com/Noble-Lab/HiCFoundation](https://github.com/Noble-Lab/HiCFoundation)

- **ARCH3D (bioRxiv, 2026)** is another 3D-genome foundation model, not an agent. It explicitly positions HiCFoundation as a patch-based baseline with a 224×224, 5-kb-resolution input window (~1.12 Mb), while pursuing longer-range global representations. [https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.02.23.707580v1.full](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.02.23.707580v1.full)

- **Benchmarks beyond GenoTEX:** BixBench has >50 real-world analysis scenarios and nearly 300 open-answer questions; its original agents achieved 17% open-answer accuracy. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00096](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00096) BioAgent Bench evaluates end-to-end RNA-seq, variant-calling, and metagenomics artifacts plus perturbation robustness (corrupt input, decoy files, prompt bloat). [https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21800](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21800) The 2026 single-cell benchmark supplies 50 real-world tasks and measures program synthesis, collaboration, execution efficiency, knowledge integration, and task quality. [https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13064268/](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13064268/) sc-HeurekaBench derives questions from validated findings in 13 Nature/Cell single-cell papers and scores dataset-grounded open answers and MCQs. [https://brbiclab.epfl.ch/projects/heurekabench-ai-co-scientist/](https://brbiclab.epfl.ch/projects/heurekabench-ai-co-scientist/)

- **Criticism is substantial.** BioAgent Bench finds that superficially completed pipelines break under controlled perturbations, so high-level workflow construction is not reliable step-level reasoning. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21800](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21800) sc-HeurekaBench reports failure to explore metadata/environment and substitution of remembered biological “facts” for actual tool-based analysis; its critic benefit depends on the underlying LLM and adds stochasticity. [https://brbiclab.epfl.ch/projects/heurekabench-ai-co-scientist/](https://brbiclab.epfl.ch/projects/heurekabench-ai-co-scientist/) An external BixBench audit argues flawed tasks and LLM-judge errors account for roughly 70% of apparent failures and can shift scores by up to 42 points. [https://generality.org/blog/posts/bixbench-broken-ceiling/](https://generality.org/blog/posts/bixbench-broken-ceiling/)

- **The credible disagreement is conditional:** BioMaster reports reliable completion on bounded, fixed workflows, whereas BixBench’s original results were poor and later audits dispute whether its scores measure agent capability at all. Thus, agents plausibly help with well-specified, containerized pipeline execution, but evidence does not establish trustworthy autonomous inference on open-ended biological questions. [https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666389926001200](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666389926001200) [https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00096](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00096) [https://generality.org/blog/posts/bixbench-broken-ceiling/](https://generality.org/blog/posts/bixbench-broken-ceiling/)

## Caveats and open questions

- I found **no direct paper/repository evidence** for an agent that iteratively fits or selects chromatin polymer, condensate, or phase-separation models against contact maps. This may be a genuine gap or a discoverability gap; absence from web search is not proof of nonexistence.

- BioMaster’s Hi-C result is a strong workflow-automation precedent, so FoldClaw should not claim “the first 3D-genomics agent.” A defensible narrower claim is: first dedicated, closed-loop **contact-data ↔ physical-simulation** agent, contingent on a systematic literature search at submission time. [https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666389926001200](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666389926001200)

- BioMaster’s reported 47/49 completion and Hi-C concordance come from the authors’ own benchmark; no independent reproduction was located. [https://github.com/ai4nucleome/BioMaster](https://github.com/ai4nucleome/BioMaster)

- Benchmark conclusions remain unstable because several use LLM judges, narrow/known datasets, proprietary changing models, or final-answer scores rather than deterministic validation of every intermediate artifact. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21800](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21800) [https://generality.org/blog/posts/bixbench-broken-ceiling/](https://generality.org/blog/posts/bixbench-broken-ceiling/)
